## What a personal year 22 means

A personal year 22 is the 4 year of the cycle with the master number kept. The tradition reads it as everything a 4 year is, work, structure, discipline and the laying of foundations with little reward until later, and then adds the 22's quality, which is scale. Where a 4 year builds the house, a 22 year is read as building the thing that will outlast the cycle: the business, the body of work, the institution, the plan with a twenty-year horizon. Numerologists call 22 the master builder and read the year accordingly, as the most productive in the system and the most pressured.

Its digits explain it. Twenty-two is the second master number, read as a 4 under pressure, and the <a href="/numbers/22/">number 22 page</a> and the <a href="/articles/master-numbers/">master numbers article</a> explain why it is kept when every other total is reduced. This page is about the year, and about why so few people ever have one.

## Why nobody has a 22 year in 2027

The arithmetic is the honest part of this page. A personal year is your reduced day plus your reduced month plus the universal year, and the largest a reduced day and month can add to is 9 + 9 = 18, for a birthday on 9, 18 or 27 September. To reach 22, the universal year has to supply at least 4. 2027 is a universal 2 (2 + 0 + 2 + 7 = 11 → 2), so the highest personal year anyone can have is 18 + 2 = 20 → 2, and nobody has a 22. 2028 is a universal 3 and the same applies.

The first chance is 2029, a universal 4 (2 + 0 + 2 + 9 = 13 → 4), when people born on 9, 18 or 27 September reach 9 + 9 + 4 = 22. In 2030, a universal 5, the 22 goes to 8, 17 and 26 September (8 + 9 + 5 = 22) and to 9, 18 and 27 August (9 + 8 + 5 = 22). The pattern continues through the decade, always to late-summer birthdays with high day totals, and then stops when the universal year drops back to 1, 2 or 3. ## What the year tends to bring

The tradition's picture of a 22 year is a 4 year with the stakes raised. Work demands more than usual and the work is larger than usual: the project that has been talked about for years is begun in earnest, the business is founded or expanded, the house is not repaired but built. Structure, routine and paperwork come to the front as they do in any 4 year, and the person is read as having, for once, the stamina for all of it.

Its shadow is the 22's, which is pressure. A 22 year can feel like a year of falling short, because the plan is large and the twelve months are not, and numerologists describe burnout and a particular paralysis in front of a project too big to start. The advice the tradition gives is the one it gives every 22. Lay one brick today, live the year as a 4, and let the master number be what the work could become rather than what it has to be by December.

## A 22 year in love

A 22 year is read as a 4 year for relationships, steady, practical and unromantic, with the 22's workload added. Couples are told the year is good for the shared project, the house, the business, the plan for the children's schooling, and poor for leisure. The person in a 22 year is building, and the partner can feel like a visitor to the site. The advice is the one the tradition gives every 22 in every pair: be home for dinner. Single people are told it is not a year for meeting people, and that anyone met in a 22 year tends to be met through the work and to be part of it.

## A 22 year at work and with money

Work is the whole content of a 22 year in the tradition's reading, and the advice is to build what will last. It is the year for founding, for the long qualification, for the systems that will run the enterprise in the 8 year. A poor year for the quick win or the job hop, since recognition for 22-year work arrives later. Money is read as a 4 year's: tight, carefully managed and mostly going into the structure, with the warning that a 22 year's ambition can lead to borrowing against a plan that will take longer than the year. None of this is financial advice.

## How to use a 22 year, if you ever have one

The tradition's advice is the 4's with one addition. Keep the routine, do the deferred things and measure the year by what is built, as in any 4 year. Then write down the large plan, in full.

A 22 year is read as the one time in the cycle when the vision and the stamina to serve it arrive together. The plan written in a 22 year is the one the next cycles are read as building.
