## What a personal year 8 means

This is the year the cycle has been working towards. The tradition reads a personal year 8 as the harvest: the year the business turns a profit, the promotion arrives, the long project is recognized and the money, for once, moves in the right direction. Numerologists describe it as the year of power, achievement and material reward, and in the same breath as a karmic year, meaning that what comes up is what was sown. An 8 year after seven years of effort is read as generous. An 8 year after seven years of avoidance is read as the bill.

The number carries the meaning. Eight is the digit of money, authority and the balance between effort and reward, and an 8 year is that digit across twelve months. The <a href="/numbers/8/">number 8 page</a> describes the digit. This page is about the year.

## How to know if 2027 is your 8 year

Reduce your day and month of birth to single digits and add them to the universal year. 2027 is 2 + 0 + 2 + 7 = 11 → 2, so 2027 is a personal year 8 for anyone whose day and month add to 6 or to 15. 5 January is 5 + 1 + 2 = 8, and 4 February is 4 + 2 + 2 = 8.

6 September is 6 + 9 + 2 = 17 → 8, and 14 January is 1 + 4 = 5, then 5 + 1 + 2 = 8. The <a href="/personal-year/">calculator</a> shows the sum for any date. This site turns the year over on 1 January.

## What the year tends to bring

The tradition's picture of an 8 year is of things coming to a head at work and in the accounts. Offers, promotions, deals, the sale of a business or a house, a raise asked for and given. Authority, too: the person in an 8 year is read as being put in charge of something, or as being seen, for the first time, as the person in charge. Public recognition is read as an 8-year event, and so is the audit.

What numerologists warn about is the 8's shadow, which is the worst of the nine in its effects. Workaholism, because the year rewards work and it is hard to stop when it is paying. Control, because authority arrives and is overused.

Materialism, measuring the year and the people in it by what they returned. And the strain on health and family of a year spent entirely at the office. The tradition's advice is the one it gives every 8: use the power lightly, and come home.

## An 8 year in love

An 8 year is not read as a romantic year, and the tradition says so. The person in it is busy, ambitious and distracted, and a partner can feel like a department of the enterprise. For couples, the advice is to make the year's success a shared one and to spend some of what arrives on the relationship rather than on the next venture.

One evening a week should stay free of work, on pain of the 9 year's reckoning. Single people are told that the 8 year is a good one for meeting someone through work and a poor one for having the time to see them. Marriage in an 8 year is read as often practical in its motives and no worse for that.

## An 8 year at work and with money

Work is the whole point of an 8 year in the tradition's reading, and the advice is to act. Ask for the raise. Negotiate the deal. Take the role that was offered and the authority that comes with it, and expand the business if the 4 and 7 years built the structure for it.

Money is read as moving more than in any other year, in both directions. It is the year of the windfall and the year of the large tax bill. The tradition's advice is to manage what arrives, since an 8 year's income spent as it comes leaves nothing for the 9 year's clearing or the 1 year's new start. None of this is financial advice, and a year of good fortune is exactly the condition in which people overreach.

## How to use an 8 year

Three things, in the tradition's reading. Act on what the 7 year concluded, because the understanding is no use unless it is cashed in. Ask for what the work is worth, since an 8 year is the one in which asking is answered. And set the terms of your own power, deciding in advance what the money is for and when the working day ends, because the year will not decide them for you.
