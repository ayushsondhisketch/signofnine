## What 3 means

Three is the number of expression. In Pythagorean numerology 1 is the self, 2 is the other, and 3 is what the two make together: a child, a conversation, a work of art, an idea said out loud. The tradition reads 3 as creativity, communication, optimism, sociability and growth, and it is the digit most often found under the angel numbers people love, since 111, 444 and 777 all reduce to it.

The associations agree. In tarot the card for 3 is the Empress, the figure of abundance and making. Its planet is Jupiter, the planet of expansion. In the Bible 3 is the Trinity, the third day, the three visitors to Abraham. Across cultures, 3 is the number of completeness in a story: beginning, middle, end.

## The strengths of 3

A 3 is heard. The tradition gives it the gifts of speech and writing, humor, imagination and the kind of optimism that makes other people want to be in the room. 3s are read as the writers, teachers, performers, salespeople and hosts, and as the friend who turns a bad evening around by talking.

## The shadow of 3

The shadow is the strength overdone: scattering, exaggeration, superficiality and the habit of starting things that never get finished because the next one is more interesting. A 3 under strain talks instead of doing. The lesson the tradition attaches is to finish one thing and show it, which is also the reading for the angel number 333.

## 3 in love

In love, 3 is playful, warm and talkative, the partner who makes the relationship fun and sometimes forgets to make it steady. The tradition's reading is that 3s fall in love easily and get bored of routine, and its advice is to stay for the dull parts. As a number people see, 333 is read as growth in a relationship through the conversation that has been postponed.

## 3 at work and with money

At work, 3 is any job where the words or the ideas are the product: writing, teaching, marketing, design, performance, sales. The tradition reads 3s as poor at administration and excellent in a room. With money, 3 is read as the spender who earns well and keeps little, with the advice to automate the saving so it happens without attention.

## 3 as an angel number and as a core number

As a repeating number, 333 is read as growth and support, and 3:33 is the most searched waking time. As a core number, 3 sits under 111, 444 and 777, three of the most-read angel numbers, which is why their readings share a thread of expression and growth. As a life path, 3 is the communicator, described on the <a href="/life-path/3/">life path 3 page</a>. As a personal year, 3 is the year of expression and social life.
