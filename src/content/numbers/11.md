## What 11 means

Eleven is the first master number. In Pythagorean numerology, almost every number is reduced to a single digit, but 11, 22 and 33 are kept when they appear as a result, because the tradition reads them as their base numbers under more pressure. Eleven is a 2, the number of partnership and sensitivity, with the volume turned up: intuition, insight, inspiration, nervous energy and the ability to sense what a room is feeling before anyone has spoken.

The associations fit the reading. In tarot the card numbered 11 in the Rider-Waite order is Justice, the figure who weighs before acting. Its planet is the Moon, as for the 2, raised.

Its shape, two 1s standing side by side, is read as a mirror, which is where the twin-flame tradition's attachment to 11:11 begins. The most common mistake is to read 11 as a double 1 (beginnings doubled). The tradition reads it as a raised 2.

## The strengths of 11

An 11 perceives. The tradition gives it intuition, empathy, inspiration, idealism and a feel for the mood of people and places. It reads 11s as the counselors, artists, teachers, healers and the person at work who knows something is wrong before the numbers show it. At its best an 11 is a 2 with an extra sense.

## The shadow of 11

The shadow is sensitivity with nowhere to go: anxiety, self-doubt, nervous exhaustion and feeling everything twice. An 11 under strain takes on the room's mood and calls it its own. The tradition's lesson is to trust the first impression and then check it, and its practical advice is the same as for every 2: say what you want, and do not carry what is not yours.

## 11 in love

In love, 11 is idealistic and easily hurt. The tradition reads 11s as partners who feel a relationship intensely and need someone steady, and its warning is the one it gives all sensitive numbers: a fear left unspoken becomes a story, and the story becomes the relationship. The advice is to say it early. As a number people see, 11:11 is the sighting most tied to soulmates and twin flames, read as a mirror of two halves.

## 11 at work and with money

At work, 11 is the inspirer and the perceiver: counseling, teaching, the arts, design, anything where reading people is the skill. The tradition reads 11s as uncomfortable with routine and brilliant in a crisis of feeling. With money, 11 is read as the 2's caution plus the 11's distraction, the number that forgets to look at the account. The advice is systems that run without attention.

## 11 in dates and as a life path

An 11 appears in a date three ways: a day of the 11th or the 29th (2 + 9 = 11), the month of November, or a life path total of 11. In each case the tradition keeps it. ## 11 as an angel number

As a sighting, 11 appears inside 11:11 and 1111, the most noticed time and the most searched sequence in the whole tradition, and inside 2222 (two 22s, the next master number).
