## What a life path 4 is like

A life path 4 is the builder. The tradition reads a 4 as someone whose life keeps returning to the same theme, which is structure: the child who lined up the toys, the colleague who actually finishes the project, the friend whose house works. Four is the square, the first stable shape, and a 4 is built to make things that stay made.

The number comes from your full date of birth. 29 January 1954 is 2 + 9 = 11 (kept as a master number), then 1, then 1 + 9 + 5 + 4 = 19 → 10 → 1, and 11 + 1 + 1 = 13 → 4. The <a href="/life-path/calculator/">calculator</a> shows the working for any date and tells you if your total is 22, which the tradition keeps. The <a href="/numbers/4/">number 4 page</a> describes the digit itself. This page is about the whole life.

## Strengths of a life path 4

The tradition is consistent about what a 4 does well. Reliability: a 4 does what it said it would do, when it said it would. Discipline, the ability to keep going after the interest has gone. Practicality, the instinct for what will actually work. And honesty, because a 4 sees no point in decoration.

Deeper than any of these is that things a 4 builds last. Companies, marriages, houses, habits: the 4 is the number of the long game, and in a culture that rewards the quick one, 4s are the people everything else quietly depends on.

## Blind spots of a life path 4

Every source names the same shadow. Rigidity: a 4 that has found a way that works does not want another. Overwork, because stopping feels like risk.

Dullness, the 4's own word for itself at its lowest. And the mistaking of routine for safety, which keeps a 4 in a job or a situation long after it should have changed. A 4 under strain says no to everything and calls it prudence.

The lesson the tradition attaches is to build and then let the building breathe. The 4s who thrive are the ones who learned to change one small thing on purpose, regularly, so that the structure stays a structure and does not become a wall.

## Life path 4 in love

In love, 4 is loyal and slow: slow to commit and, once committed, steady for life. The tradition reads 4s as the partners who show love by doing, the bill paid, the shelf fixed, the plan made, and who need to be told that saying it also counts. The 4's gift to a relationship is that it stays. The warning is that staying can become stasis.

The pairings the tradition favors are 2, 6, 7 and 8. The 2 is a partner who values the steadiness and softens it. The 6 is shared commitment to home, the pair most likely to marry and stay.

Then the 7 is depth that the 4 respects and quiet that it does not mind, and the 8 is two builders who respect effort. The demanding pairs are 1, 3, 5 and 9, all of which move faster than a 4 likes and read the 4's pace as resistance. Every pair is worked through on the <a href="/compatibility/4/">life path 4 compatibility page</a>.

## Life path 4 careers and money

Work that fits a 4 is the long project and the craft: engineering, accounting, building trades, administration, farming, law, operations, anything that rewards patience and punishes shortcuts. The tradition reads 4s as excellent employees and careful bosses, and as miserable in jobs that change every week.

With money, 4 is the saver and the budgeter, the number of the pension and the emergency fund. The risk is hoarding against a disaster that never comes and never spending on the present. The advice is to budget for enjoyment as a line item, which a 4 will do if it is in the plan. None of this is financial advice.

## Famous people with life path 4

Dates that reduce to 4 include:

- Bill Gates (28 October 1955): 1 + 1 + 2 = 4
- Elton John (25 March 1947): 7 + 3 + 3 = 13 → 4
- Marie Curie (7 November 1867): 7 + 11 + 22 = 40 → 4
- Keanu Reeves (2 September 1964): 2 + 9 + 2 = 13 → 4
- Brad Pitt (18 December 1963): 9 + 3 + 1 = 13 → 4

The tradition points to the builders of large things in the list. The names are here as worked examples of the arithmetic.

## Life path 4 and the master number 22

Some dates reduce not to 4 but to 22, the master builder, which the tradition reads as a 4 with a plan the size of a city. If your working ends in 22 rather than 4, read the <a href="/life-path/22/">life path 22 page</a> as well as this one. The tradition's own advice to 22s is that they live as 4s most of the time, building steadily, and that the master number is the ceiling rather than the floor.
