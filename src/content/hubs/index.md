## What angel numbers are

An angel number is a sequence that catches the eye: three or four of the same digit, a mirror such as 1221, a run such as 1234, or a time such as 11:11. The angel-number tradition reads each as a message, and the meanings come from older numerology: 1 is beginnings, 5 is change, 8 is money, and a repeated digit is that meaning at full strength. The tradition is modern. It begins with Doreen Virtue's *Angel Numbers* (2005) and *Angel Numbers 101* (2008), which is where almost every list online, including this one, gets its readings.

This site treats the readings as readings. Every number page shows the arithmetic (5 + 5 + 5 = 15 → 6), names the tradition it quotes, and explains before anything else why a number seems to follow you.

## Why a number seems to follow you

Once a number has your attention, your brain flags every later sighting and skips the thousands of other numbers you passed. Psychologists call this the frequency illusion, and it is strong enough that people are often surprised when they count. Clock times add to it: 4:44 comes round twice a day, and if you check your phone at the same point in your routine you will meet it often.

None of that makes the sighting useless. Most people who look up a number already know what the number is "about" in their own life. The reading gives them a way to name it.

## How to read any number

Three steps, the same on every page here. First, the digits: what each one means and how often it appears. Second, the sum: add the digits and reduce to a single digit, keeping 11, 22 and 33, to find the core number underneath. Third, the context: a clock time, a date, a number plate, the thing you were thinking about. The lookup box at the top of the site does the first two steps for any number, including ones without a page.
