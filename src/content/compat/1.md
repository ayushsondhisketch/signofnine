## How a life path 1 loves

The leader loves the way it does everything else: first, fast and on its own terms. A 1 falls hard and decides quickly, and the tradition reads its loyalty as fierce once given. The difficulty is in the daily running of a relationship, where a 1 is used to deciding and a partner is not a project. Numerologists describe the 1 in love as generous with action and stingy with attention, the partner who will fix your car and forget to ask about your day.

Every rating below follows the Pythagorean pairing table, which is set out in full on the <a href="/compatibility/">compatibility guide</a>. They describe where a pair starts, not where it has to finish. ## What a 1 needs from a partner

Room, respect and a reason to come home. The tradition reads the 1 as needing a partner who admires the drive without competing with it. The partner needs a life of their own, so the 1 does not feel responsible for two, and the ability to say "stop" and be heard. The pairs that go wrong for a 1 are the ones where the partner either fights for the wheel or disappears behind the 1 entirely. The pairs that go right leave the 1 in front and make it want to turn around.
