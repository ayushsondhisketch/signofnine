## How a life path 11 loves

The intuitive loves the way a 2 does, by attention and devotion, with everything felt twice. An 11 knows what its partner is feeling before the partner does, remembers every word, and lies awake over both. The tradition reads the 11 in love as idealistic, loyal and easily hurt, the partner who needs a steady hand more than an exciting one. Its chief danger is the fear left unspoken, which becomes a story, which becomes the relationship.

## What an 11 needs from a partner

Steadiness, plain words and a reason to trust the first impression. The tradition reads the 11 as needing a partner whose calm is real, who says what they mean so the 11 does not have to guess, and who treats the 11's perceptions as information rather than drama. The pairs that go wrong for an 11 are the ones where the partner is unpredictable, dismissive of feelings, or so sensitive themselves that nobody holds the ground. The pairs that go right let the 11 feel everything and give it somewhere solid to stand while it does.
