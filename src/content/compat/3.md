## How a life path 3 loves

The communicator loves out loud. A 3 is affectionate, funny, flirtatious and expressive, and it needs a partner who enjoys the performance and can also tell when the performance has stopped. The tradition reads the 3 in love as warm and easily bored, the number most likely to leave a relationship that has gone quiet and most likely to regret it. Its gift to a partner is that life with a 3 is rarely dull. Its cost is that a 3 can scatter its attention across a room and leave the person at home feeling like the audience.

## What a 3 needs from a partner

An audience, a floor and a sense of humor. The tradition reads the 3 as needing a partner who laughs at the jokes, who provides enough stability that the 3 does not have to, and who can take the occasional sharp line without keeping it. The pairs that go wrong for a 3 are the ones where the partner wants it to be more serious, or more consistent, or quieter, and says so often. The pairs that go right let the 3 be the light in the house and quietly make sure the bills are paid.
