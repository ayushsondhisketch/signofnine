## How a life path 4 loves

The builder loves by showing up. A 4 is loyal, dependable and present, the partner who remembers the dates, pays the bills and is where it said it would be. The tradition reads the 4 in love as slow to commit and then committed for good, and as expressing affection in deeds rather than words, which partners who want the words can miss. Its difficulty is rigidity: a 4 likes life to run a certain way and can experience a partner's spontaneity as a threat to the structure.

The paragraph for each says where the fit is easy and where it takes work. ## What a 4 needs from a partner

Reliability, respect for the plan, and someone to make it change. The tradition reads the 4 as needing a partner who keeps their word, who does not mock the schedule, and who can introduce the new thing the 4 would never arrange and make it feel safe. The pairs that go wrong for a 4 are the ones where the partner is unreliable, or treats the 4's steadiness as dullness, or wants to rebuild the structure every month. The pairs that go right let the 4 be the foundation and remind it that foundations are for building on.
