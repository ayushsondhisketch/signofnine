## How a life path 33 loves

The master teacher loves the way a 6 does, by doing, noticing, feeding and staying, with the care widened to everyone and the risk widened with it. A 33 can love the whole world and make its partner feel like one more person on the list. The tradition reads that as the whole difficulty: the partner of a 33 is loved and looked after and may still feel unseen. Numerologists give the 33 in love the same advice they give the 6, with one addition: keep something back, and keep it for the person at home.

## What a 33 needs from a partner

Someone who looks after the carer. The tradition reads the 33 as needing a partner who notices when it is empty and says so, and who does not compete with the people it cares for. The partner also has to be able to accept care without feeling like a patient. 

The pairs that go wrong for a 33 are the ones where the partner needs nothing or resents the 33's wider life. They also go wrong when the partner lets the 33 run the relationship as one more thing to manage. The pairs that go right make the 33 the one who is looked after, at least some of the time, and insist on it.
