## How a life path 9 loves

The humanitarian loves generously and a little diffusely. A 9 is warm, forgiving, romantic in the old sense and drawn to causes, and the tradition reads its difficulty as scale: a 9 can love humanity and neglect the person at the table. Numerologists describe the 9 in love as the partner who will forgive you anything and may not notice you were there, and as the number most in need of being reminded that one person is also a cause.

Every rating below follows the Pythagorean pairing table set out in full on the <a href="/compatibility/">compatibility guide</a>. A rating is where a pair starts. The paragraph for each pair says where the fit is easy and where it takes work. ## What a 9 needs from a partner

Shared purpose, patience and someone practical. The tradition reads the 9 as needing a partner who respects what it cares about, who does not resent the hours given to other people, and who quietly makes sure the household runs while the 9 is saving the world. The pairs that go wrong for a 9 are the ones where the partner competes with the causes or wants the 9 to be smaller. The pairs that go right join the 9's wider life and ask, now and then, for some of the compassion at home.
