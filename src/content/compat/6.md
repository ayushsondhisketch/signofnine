## How a life path 6 loves

The caretaker loves by doing. A 6 remembers, cooks, notices, plans and stays, and it reads a partner's willingness to be looked after as a form of closeness. The tradition's reading is that a 6 is the easiest number to live with and one of the hardest to live up to, because the care comes with an unspoken expectation that it will be returned in kind. Partners who show care differently, by giving space or by providing, can leave a 6 feeling unloved while doing everything right.

Every match below follows the Pythagorean tradition's table of temperaments, which is published in full on the <a href="/compatibility/">compatibility guide</a>. A rating is where the reading starts, not where it ends. The paragraph for each pair says where the fit is easy and where it takes work. Work out both numbers with the <a href="/compatibility/calculator/">calculator</a> if you are not sure of your partner's.

## What a 6 needs from a partner

Three things, in the tradition's reading: to be needed, to be thanked, and to be given something to do for the relationship that the partner could not do alone. The pairs that go wrong for a 6 are the ones where the partner needs nothing, says nothing, or does everything, and the 6 is left with its care and nowhere to put it. The pairs that go right let the 6 care without letting it run everything.
