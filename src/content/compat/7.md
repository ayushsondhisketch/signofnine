## How a life path 7 loves

The seeker loves quietly and from a slight distance, and the tradition reads that as the whole difficulty. A 7 is loyal, thoughtful and faithful, and it needs more solitude than almost any partner finds easy, so it is often taken for cold when it is only private. Numerologists describe the 7 in love as slow to open, deep once open, and inclined to retreat into its own head when a relationship gets loud. Its gift is a partner who thinks about you carefully. Its cost is that you may have to ask what it concluded.

## What a 7 needs from a partner

Space, trust and a mind worth talking to. The tradition reads the 7 as needing a partner who does not take the closed door personally, who has a full life of their own, and who can hold a real conversation when the door opens. The pairs that go wrong for a 7 are the ones where the partner needs constant company, reads silence as a message, or finds the 7's interests odd. The pairs that go right let the 7 be alone and make it want to come out.
