## How a life path 8 loves

The achiever loves by providing. An 8 is loyal, generous with money and effort, and protective of the people it has taken responsibility for. The tradition reads its difficulty as control: an 8 is used to running things and a relationship is not a thing to run. Numerologists describe the 8 in love as the partner who will give you everything except its evenings, and who is surprised to be told that the evenings were the point.

## What an 8 needs from a partner

Loyalty, respect and someone who will make it come home. The tradition reads the 8 as needing a partner who admires what it builds without being dazzled by it, and who is not in competition for the top job. The partner also has to be able to insist on a life outside the work and be heard. The pairs that go wrong for an 8 are the ones where the partner fights it for control, dismisses its ambition, or quietly resents the hours. The pairs that go right let the 8 run the business and make the home somewhere it wants to be.
