## How a life path 2 loves

The peacemaker loves by paying attention. A 2 notices the mood before the words, remembers what was said and what was not, and will give way on almost anything to keep the peace. The tradition reads it as the number most suited to partnership and most at risk inside one, because a 2 can spend years keeping a relationship smooth and never say what it wants. Numerologists describe the 2 in love as devoted, tactful and quietly keeping score.

Every rating below comes from the Pythagorean pairing table, which is set out in full on the <a href="/compatibility/">compatibility guide</a>. They are a starting point, and the paragraph for each pair says where the fit is easy and where it takes work. The <a href="/compatibility/calculator/">calculator</a> gives both life paths from two dates of birth, and the <a href="/life-path/2/">life path 2 page</a> describes the peacemaker on its own.

## What a 2 needs from a partner

Reassurance, presence and permission to want things. The tradition reads the 2 as needing a partner who is steady enough to lean on and who notices the thousand small things the 2 does. The partner also has to ask the 2 what it wants, and refuse "whatever you like" as an answer. The pairs that go wrong for a 2 are the ones where the partner is absent, dismissive or so dominant that the 2 vanishes into their life. The pairs that go right give the 2 someone to care for who cares back in a currency the 2 can read.
