## How a life path 5 loves

The free spirit loves with enthusiasm and leaves when it feels caged. A 5 is charming, curious, physical and quick to fall. The tradition reads its difficulty as commitment: not that a 5 cannot commit, but that it has to choose to, and it has to be able to feel free inside the choice. Numerologists describe the 5 in love as the number most likely to be accused of restlessness and most likely to be loyal once it has found a relationship with room in it.

Every rating below follows the Pythagorean pairing table set out in full on the <a href="/compatibility/">compatibility guide</a>. A rating is where a pair starts, and the paragraph for each says where the fit is easy and where it takes work. The <a href="/compatibility/calculator/">calculator</a> gives both life paths from two dates of birth. The <a href="/life-path/5/">life path 5 page</a> describes the free spirit on its own.

## What a 5 needs from a partner

Room, trust and a reason to come back. The tradition reads the 5 as needing a partner who does not read absence as a message, who has a full life of their own, and who makes home interesting enough that the 5 wants to be there. The pairs that go wrong for a 5 are the ones where the partner waits up, keeps count or tries to schedule the 5's freedom. The pairs that go right give the 5 the open door and make it the one it walks back through.
