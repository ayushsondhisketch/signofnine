## How a life path 22 loves

The master builder loves the way a 4 does, steadily and for good, with the one complication that the work comes first and the relationship gets what is left. A 22 is loyal, dependable and committed once committed. The tradition reads its difficulty as scale: a 22 is building something that will outlast it, and a partner can feel like a visitor to the site. Numerologists describe the 22 in love in the same terms as an ambitious 8, and give it the same advice, which is to be home for dinner.

## What a 22 needs from a partner

Belief in the plan, patience with the hours, and someone who will make it stop. The tradition reads the 22 as needing a partner who takes the vision seriously and says so, who does not experience the workload as a rejection, and who can insist on an evening off and be obeyed. The pairs that go wrong for a 22 are the ones where the partner competes with the project, mocks it or quietly resents it. The pairs that go right are inside the plan and make the home the part of it that is already built.
