## What the soul urge number is

Three of the five core numbers come from your name, and they split the name three ways. The expression number takes every letter. The personality number takes the consonants.

The soul urge number takes the vowels, and the tradition reads it as the quietest of the three: not what you can do and not how you come across, but what you want. Numerologists since L. Dow Balliett have called it the heart's desire for that reason, and the two names are used interchangeably.

Because it is read as a motive rather than a talent, the soul urge is the number people most often recognize themselves in and least often show. A soul urge 7 in a busy, sociable life is read as someone who goes home and wants silence. A soul urge 1 in a supporting role is read as someone who wants, privately, to be in charge.

## How to calculate your soul urge number

Write out your full name as it appears on your birth certificate. Pick out the vowels: A, E, I, O and U, plus Y when no other vowel sits beside it in the syllable. Give each its value on the Pythagorean chart, which assigns 1 to 9 to the alphabet in order and starts again at J and S. A is 1, E is 5, I is 9, O is 6, U is 3, and Y is 7. Add the values and reduce the total to a single digit, keeping 11, 22 and 33 if the reduction lands on one.

Take Mary Louise Carter again, the name used on the expression number page. The vowels are A and Y in Mary (1 + 7), O, U, I and E in Louise (6 + 3 + 9 + 5), and A and E in Carter (1 + 5). Together they come to 37, then 3 + 7 = 10, then 1. Mary's soul urge number is 1. The calculator at the top of this page does the same for any name and lists the letters it counted, so a disputed Y is visible rather than hidden.

There is a check built into the system. The vowels and the consonants of a name add up to the expression total: Mary's vowels make 37, her consonants make 40, and 37 + 40 is the 77 the expression page arrived at. If your three name numbers do not reconcile that way, a letter has been miscounted.

## What each soul urge number means

The meanings are the nine digit meanings turned inward, read as wants rather than as abilities. A soul urge 1 wants independence and to lead. 2 wants peace, partnership and to be needed. 3 wants to express itself and be heard.

4 wants order, security and something solid to build. 5 wants freedom, variety and experience. 6 wants a home and people to look after. 7 wants understanding, privacy and time to think.

8 wants achievement, authority and material security. 9 wants to be of use to something larger than itself. The master numbers 11, 22 and 33 are read as the wants of 2, 4 and 6 with more urgency behind them. The <a href="/numbers/">number meanings</a> pages give each in full.

The tradition's advice for reading it is to notice where the want is not being met. A soul urge 5 in a settled routine, or a soul urge 6 living alone far from family, is read as the source of a restlessness the person may not have named.

## Reading the soul urge with the expression number

Numerologists read the three name numbers as a set. The expression is what you can do, the personality is what people see first, and the soul urge is what you are after. Mary, with an expression 5, a soul urge 1 and a personality 4, would be read as a persuasive, adaptable person who comes across as steady and reliable and privately wants to run her own show. Whether that describes any real Mary is for her to say. The tradition offers it as a frame, and the honest writers say so.

## Where the vowel method comes from

The soul urge calculation belongs to the Pythagorean system as it was assembled in America around 1900 by L. Dow Balliett and then set out in Juno Jordan's books in the 1960s. The older Chaldean method values letters differently and does not usually split the name by vowels and consonants. The differences are explained in the article on <a href="/articles/pythagorean-vs-chaldean/">Pythagorean and Chaldean numerology</a>.
