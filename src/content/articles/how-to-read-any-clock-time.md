## Start with the sum

The tradition reads any number, on a clock or anywhere else, by reducing it to a single digit, and a clock time is no different. Add the digits and keep adding until one is left. 10:47 is 1 + 0 + 4 + 7 = 12, then 1 + 2 = 3. The core number is 3, and the first and main reading of 10:47 is the reading of 3: expression, creativity, sociability and the risk of scattering, described in full on the <a href="/numbers/3/">number 3 page</a>.

A few more: 9:15 is 15 → 6, the home and care number. 16:16 is 14 → 5, change. 23:23 is 10 → 1, beginnings. 18:45 is 18 → 9, endings and the wide view.

This is the whole method in one step, and it works for every time of day. The <a href="/lookup/">lookup tool</a> on this site does it for any time or number you type and lays out the digit meanings beside the sum. That is why the site does not have a separate page for each of the 1,440 minutes in a day. Most of them have never been searched for, and a page for each would say the same thing this article says once.

## Then read the parts

Numerologists add two more layers for anyone who wants them. The hour is read as one number and the minutes as another, so the time becomes a small sentence. For 10:47, the hour 10 reduces to 1, the number of starting, and the minutes 47 reduce to 11, the master number the tradition reads as a 2 with more feeling, partnership and patience. Read together through the tradition's lens, 10:47 is a beginning that will need other people, with the 3 over the top of it. That is a reading, not a fact, and it is built the way the tradition builds every reading: digit by digit, from the <a href="/numbers/">meanings of 1 to 9</a>.

The second layer is the shape. Repeated digits, 11:11, 4:44, 22:22, are read as the digit's quality intensified, and each has its own page in the <a href="/angel-numbers/times/">clock times</a> section. Mirrored times, 12:21, 21:12, 10:01, are read as reflection, a prompt to look at where you are rather than where you are going. Both 12:21 and 21:12 add to 6.

Sequences, 12:34 and 1:23, are read as progress and order, things falling into place in the right sequence. 12:34 adds to 10 → 1. Doubled pairs, 10:10 and 12:12, are read as the pair's number emphasized, so 10:10 is read as a double 1 with the 0's open space between, reducing to 2. A time with no pattern, which is most of them, is read on its sum alone.

## The 12-hour and 24-hour question

Which digits you add depends on the clock you saw, and the tradition's answer is to read what you saw. 22:22 on a 24-hour display adds to 8, the achievement number. The same moment on a 12-hour display is 10:22, which adds to 5. Neither is more correct.

The sighting was of a particular display and the attention was caught by particular digits. Readers who want a single reading for a moment regardless of the clock usually settle on their own clock's format and keep to it. Midnight is the awkward case, because 00:00 adds to 0, which is outside the system of 1 to 9. The <a href="/numbers/0/">number 0 page</a> explains what numerologists make of zero and why most readings of midnight fall back on 12:00, which adds to 3.

## The honest reason you keep seeing it

Before any of the above, check the routine. A time that recurs is usually a time you look at the clock. Most people look at the clock at the same points every day: on waking, when the kettle boils, at the end of a meeting, at the start of a lunch break, in bed. If 10:47 is the time you reach the office or the time the baby wakes, the number is reporting your schedule, and the schedule is the reading.

Then there is the frequency illusion, which every angel number page on this site describes and the article on <a href="/articles/why-you-keep-seeing-repeating-numbers/">why you keep seeing repeating numbers</a> covers in full. Once 10:47 has your attention, every 10:47 is counted and every 10:46 is not, so the time seems to be following you. It is not. You are noticing it. That is not a reason to dismiss the question of what was on your mind when you looked, which is the useful question, and the one the tradition's reading comes down to as well.

## A worked example from start to finish

Suppose you have seen 7:38 three times this week and want to know what to make of it. The sum: 7 + 3 + 8 = 18 → 9. The core reading is the 9's: completion, letting go, the wide view, and the tradition would say you are near the end of something.

The parts: the hour 7 is the thinker's number, and the minutes 38 reduce to 11, read as a 2 with more feeling, partnership. Through the tradition's lens, 7:38 reads as an ending that needs thinking through and talking over. No pattern, so nothing to add from the shape.

Now the honest check. 7:38 is roughly when a great many people are leaving the house. If that is your time, you are seeing it because it is when you look at your phone with your keys in your hand, and the sighting means you are on time. If it is not tied to a habit, write down what you were thinking the next time you see it, and do that for two weeks. The notes will tell you more than the digits, and the digits were only ever a way of making you look.
